An adventure across the lands of Numeria, where every monster falls to the power of maths.

Your Quest

Travel through six regions of Numeria, each guarded by a monster that only maths can defeat. In every battle, answer the questions correctly to strike your foe — get one wrong and it strikes back!

  • 💥 Correct answers damage the monster and earn gems.
  • ❤️ Wrong answers cost you a heart — lose them all and you're defeated.
  • 🔥 Chain correct answers for streak bonuses.
  • 🗺️ Defeat a region's guardian to unlock the next on the map.
  • 🐉 Clear all five regions to reach Dragon's Spire and face the final boss.

🔊 Sound effects included — use the toggle in-game to mute.
